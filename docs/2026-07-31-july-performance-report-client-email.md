### What We Delivered in July

**Rewrote four of your location pages: Barking, Hornchurch, Basildon and Greenwich.** These were the four pages generating the most impressions at the weakest positions. Each now carries genuinely local detail: the property stock in the area, the named neighbourhoods we cover, the local commercial base, and the security requirements that particular mix of buildings creates. Previously these pages shared almost all of their wording with the other 25 town pages, which gave Google very little reason to rank them.

**Gave every one of those towns its own page title and description in search results.** This turned out to matter more than we expected. Your Hornchurch page already ranks 4th for searches such as "fire detection system Ardleigh Green" and was earning no clicks at all from them, because the result Google displayed was titled simply "Security Systems Hornchurch". Someone searching for their own neighbourhood had no reason to think the page was about their area. Those pages now name the area and the postcode.

**Published a guide to Martyn's Law,** the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025, positioning J&L on a subject that will affect a large number of commercial premises across Essex and Greater London.

One correction worth passing on. In June's report I described a Spring 2027 deadline for premises with a capacity over 200. Having checked this against the Government's statutory guidance, the position is more nuanced and you should know the accurate version. No commencement date has been announced for the duties on premises. There is a commitment to an implementation period of at least 24 months from Royal Assent in April 2025, but no date is fixed in law. The regime also has two tiers: premises for 200 to 799 people fall into a standard tier, where the duties are largely about procedures, and only premises for 800 or more fall into the enhanced tier, where physical security measures come into play. The article states this accurately. It is worth being aware of, because some suppliers are using a specific deadline to create urgency.

**Published a commercial fire alarm cost guide,** aimed at business buyers who are comparing quotes. It explains what actually drives the price of a commercial system, the category, the device count, the building itself, and the ongoing servicing obligation, and directs the reader to a free site survey rather than quoting installation prices that could not be accurate without seeing the premises.

**Fixed a problem with your BS 5839 content.** Google was showing your HMO landlord article for searches about the BS 5839 standard itself, where it ranked between 20th and 45th, instead of showing your dedicated standards explainer. The two articles were competing with each other. We have corrected the links between them so each targets the searches it should.

**Added illustrations to five of your articles,** including the three fire risk assessment guides published at the end of June, which went live without them.

**Made three accuracy corrections** found during the work: wording in one article that could have been read as claiming an accreditation J&L does not hold, a line that did not make sufficiently clear that fire risk assessments are arranged through a qualified assessor rather than covered by your BAFE accreditation, and some incorrect article length data.
