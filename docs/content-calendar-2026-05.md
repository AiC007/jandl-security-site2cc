## 1. Content Gap Analysis

### 1.1 What is working

Two blog posts are generating nearly all the site's organic visibility:

- `/blog/hmo-fire-alarm-requirements-bs5839`: 669 impressions, 4 clicks, avg position 12.3. Dominant asset. Competing on page 1 for six HMO fire alarm regulatory queries. Not fully optimised.
- `/blog/business-cctv-ico-compliance-uk-2026`: 45 impressions, 0 clicks, avg position 9.4. Ranking well but no click-through. Title/meta may need work.

**Pattern confirmed:** compliance and regulation content performs. The audience (HMO landlords, business owners, facilities managers) is searching for exactly this. J&L has a credibility advantage: SSAIB-approved, BAFE-certified, hands-on installer. No competitor in the HMO space is an NSI-approved fire installer with field evidence.

### 1.2 What is not working

**Location landing pages (50 pages, zero impressions from 48 of them):**
The matrix is indexed but not ranking. The content is too generic. Queries like "fire alarm installer brentwood" (82 impressions, position 72 on the homepage) have no dedicated destination. The homepage cannot compete with a location-specific page for this intent.

**Branded search (0 clicks post-migration):**
"j&l security" and "j&l alarms" impressions exist but no clicks. This is a migration artefact and likely a Google Business Profile/SERP layout issue, not a content problem. Priority: diagnose the SERP manually.

**No commercial intent content:**
No page currently targets "how much does a burglar alarm cost", "CCTV installation price", or "fire alarm maintenance contract Essex" type queries, which are the highest-converting searches from buyers with budget and intent.

## 3. Content Production Standards

Every piece of content produced for J&L must meet these standards:

- UK English throughout. No emojis. No em dashes.
- Minimum 800 words for location pages; 1,500 for blog posts.
- Every post/page must include: target query in H1, in first paragraph, in at least one subheading.
- FAQ block on every page (minimum 4 questions targeting long-tail variants).
- Certification badges (SSAIB, BAFE, CHAS, FIA) on every service and location page.
- Both phone numbers in every CTA section (0204 538 5925 and 0208 220 4770).
- Internal links: minimum 3 per page (to related service, location, and contact).
- BlogPosting or Service schema on every new page, server-rendered JSON-LD.
- Update sitemap and llms.txt after each publish.
