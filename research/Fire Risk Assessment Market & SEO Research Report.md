# Fire Risk Assessment Market & SEO Research Report
**Prepared for:** J&L Security (jandlsecurity.co.uk)  
**Date:** May 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

This report details a comprehensive market and SEO analysis of the Fire Risk Assessment (FRA) sector in Essex and Greater London. J&L Security currently offers FRAs but only as an ancillary service mentioned briefly on the fire alarms page. The research reveals a significant missed commercial opportunity. There is strong, consistent search demand for FRAs, driven by stringent UK legal requirements, particularly the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and the recent Building Safety Act 2022. Competitors in the region are successfully capturing this traffic through dedicated, location-optimised landing pages. By restructuring the website to feature a dedicated FRA service page and leveraging existing BAFE and SSAIB accreditations, J&L Security can effectively compete for high-intent commercial and landlord traffic.

A review of the current J&L Security website (jandlsecurity.co.uk) identifies critical gaps in the presentation of FRA services.

### The Missing Dedicated Page
Currently, FRAs are not listed in the main navigation or the homepage services dropdown. They are only mentioned as a secondary service on the Fire Alarms page: *"We also support clients with fire risk assessments..."* [5]. 

This structure presents severe SEO limitations. Without a dedicated URL (e.g., `/services/fire-risk-assessments`), search engines cannot easily index and rank the site for FRA-specific queries. ## 6. Regulatory and Legal Context

The demand for FRAs is entirely driven by UK legislation. Understanding and communicating these laws is crucial for marketing the service.

### The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 (FSO)
This is the foundational legislation. It mandates that any person who has some level of control in premises must take reasonable steps to reduce the risk from fire. This "Responsible Person" (typically an employer, owner, or landlord) is legally required to carry out and regularly review a fire risk assessment for all non-domestic premises, including the communal areas of HMOs and blocks of flats [6].

### Recent Legislative Changes
Following the Grenfell Tower Inquiry, fire safety laws have been significantly tightened, increasing the burden on landlords and building owners:
*   **Fire Safety Act 2021:** Clarified that for multi-occupied residential buildings, the fire risk assessment must include the building's structure, external walls (including cladding and balconies), and flat entrance doors [7].
*   **Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022:** Introduced new duties for responsible persons of high-rise residential buildings, including providing information to fire and rescue services and undertaking regular checks of fire doors [8].
*   **Building Safety Act 2022 (Section 156):** Effective October 2023, this requires all Responsible Persons to record their fire risk assessment in full, regardless of the number of employees, and enhances requirements for cooperation between multiple responsible persons in a building [9].

## 7. Content Structure Recommendations

To capture the identified search demand, J&L Security must create a dedicated Fire Risk Assessment service page. The recommended structure for this page is as follows:

### Recommended Page Architecture: `/services/fire-risk-assessments`

1.  **Hero Section (Above the Fold):**
    *   **Headline:** BAFE-Certified Fire Risk Assessments in Essex & London.
    *   **Sub-headline:** Comprehensive, legally compliant PAS79 fire risk assessments for commercial premises, landlords, and HMOs.
    *   **Call to Action (CTA):** "Book a Free Survey" or "Get a Quote Today".
    *   **Trust Signals:** Prominently display BAFE, SSAIB, and CHAS logos.

2.  **Introduction & Legal Requirement:**
    *   Briefly explain what an FRA is.
    *   Highlight the legal obligation under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and the Building Safety Act 2022. Emphasise the risks of non-compliance (fines, invalid insurance).

3.  **Who Needs an Assessment? (Targeting Keywords):**
    *   Commercial Businesses & Offices.
    *   Landlords & HMOs (specifically targeting "fire risk assessment HMO landlord").
    *   Blocks of Flats & Residential Management Companies.

4.  **The J&L Security Assessment Process:**
    *   Step-by-step breakdown: Site visit, hazard identification, evaluating people at risk, evaluating existing fire protection (alarms, lighting, doors), and delivering the final PAS79-compliant digital report.

5.  **Pricing Guidance (Addressing Intent):**
    *   While exact prices may require a survey, providing starting prices (e.g., "HMO assessments from £X", "Commercial assessments from £Y") directly answers the high-volume "cost" queries and filters out low-quality leads. Industry averages range from £150 for small low-risk premises to £500+ for mid-level commercial buildings [10].

6.  **Why Choose Us (Differentiation):**
    *   Emphasise the advantage of using a company that can both *assess* the risk and *install/maintain* the solutions (alarms, emergency lighting) under one roof.
    *   Highlight BAFE certification as proof of competence.

7.  **Service Areas:**
    *   List key target locations in Essex and Greater London to build local relevance.

8.  **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):**
    *   Address common queries: "How often should it be reviewed?", "Can I do it myself?", "What is a Responsible Person?".
