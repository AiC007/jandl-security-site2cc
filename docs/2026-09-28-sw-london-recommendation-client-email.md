Dear Jag,

Yes, these areas are worth covering, but not with ten new pages. We recommend adding the neighbourhoods to your existing Fulham, Battersea and Streatham pages, plus two new pages: Clapham, and Chelsea and Kensington.

**Why not ten pages**

Few people search area by area in south-west London. Of the searches we checked, only "burglar alarm Clapham" registers, at about 10 a month. Your Fulham, Battersea and Streatham pages, live since May, currently sit on the second page of Google. Ten more pages of the same kind would most likely sit there too, and Google can treat a run of near-identical area pages as low quality.

**What we will do**

- **Fulham:** add Parsons Green, West Brompton and Chelsea Harbour.
- **Battersea:** add Nine Elms, Vauxhall and South Lambeth.
- **Streatham:** add Brixton Hill, Tulse Hill and Clapham Park.
- **New Clapham page,** also covering Stockwell, Oval and Balham.
- **New Chelsea and Kensington page,** also covering Brompton, Earl's Court, South Kensington and Knightsbridge.

Naming neighbourhoods works. Your Fulham page already appears on the first page of Google for burglar alarms in Sands End, a neighbourhood it mentions.

> **One thing we found.** Eight of your existing area pages are not yet in Google at all: Brentwood, Romford, Ilford, Dagenham, Redbridge, Canary Wharf, Harlow and Epping. We will find out why and fix that first, as it is worth more than any new page.

We will go ahead on this basis and confirm when the changes are live.
